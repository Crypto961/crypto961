As of this writing, Crypto961 does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind. We don't currently set any first-party cookies either — the site is static and doesn't use browser storage to remember visitors.

What this page will cover once that changes

If we add analytics (to understand traffic) or advertising (per our monetization plans), this page will be updated to list, before launch:

  • Every cookie/tracker by name, purpose, and duration
  • Whether it's strictly necessary, functional, analytics, or advertising
  • A consent banner will appear for EEA/UK visitors (and other jurisdictions that require it), defaulting to "off" for anything non-essential, with equal-weight Accept/Reject options

Submitting the newsletter form sends your email to Formspree for processing — see our Privacy Policy for details. This is a direct form submission, not a tracking cookie.
